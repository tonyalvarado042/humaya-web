Wellness

The body first.
Everything else follows.

Cold, heat, light and movement. Cold plunge, sauna, pool with a view of the volcano, infrared light and gym — all in one place at the foot of the Arenal Volcano, with the hot springs nearby, so you leave different from how you arrived.

A wellness hotel is not a hotel with a spa.

The difference is the order of things. In most hotels, wellness is a service you buy separately, at a set time, if there is room left. At Humaya it is the reason the place exists: everything is steps from the villa where you sleep, open, with no appointment to book and nowhere to drive.

01 · Cold

Cold plunge

Cold immersion is the centre of everything else. You get in, you breathe, you get out. You don't need to arrive knowing how: if it's your first time, someone stays with you; if you've done it for years, nobody interrupts.

02 · Heat

Sauna

Wood, volcanic stone and a window into the forest. The sauna is the other half of the cold plunge: what makes the cold feel good instead of feeling like punishment.

03 · Water

Pool with a view of the volcano

A pool with the Arenal right in front of it. No excursion, no entrance fee, no opening hours: it's there when you want it.

04 · Light

Infrared light therapy

What you can't see. Infrared light works where the muscle can't reach on its own, and it's what keeps a hard day on the mountain from charging you the next one.

05 · Movement

Gym

Properly equipped, not three machines in a hallway. Because Move is the first of the five pillars, and the body comes first, always.

06 · Nearby

Hot springs nearby

The Arenal has been heating this water for millennia, and it is the reason La Fortuna is La Fortuna. It is not on the property: the volcano heats the water, we just tell you where. There are dozens five or ten minutes away, and we book them for you.

Cold, heat, and again.

None does alone what they do together. The contrast between the cold plunge and the heat of the sauna, the pool afterwards, infrared light when the muscle can't get there on its own: that is what the body understands.

There is no required sequence and no class you have to be on time for. There is an order that works, and someone to explain it on your first day if you want.

Frequently asked questions.

What does the wellness area include?
Cold plunge, sauna, pool with a view of the volcano, infrared light therapy and gym, plus the trails and the garden: all of that is where you sleep. The hot springs are not ours — they are nearby, five or ten minutes away, and there are dozens to choose from. We help you choose and book.
Do I need to know about this to use it?
No. Nobody has to arrive knowing. The team walks you through your first cold immersion, and leaves you alone if you've been doing it for years.
Where exactly is it?
In La Fortuna de San Carlos, province of Alajuela, Costa Rica, at the foot of the Arenal Volcano.
When does Humaya open?
In December 2026. The first reservations can already be made online.
