Our purpose

Care for life and open opportunities.

Our purpose also embraces the place that receives us and the people who call it home. We want HUMAYA's growth to help restore nature and open opportunities for our community.

We start with 1% of our revenue.

Not to a generic cause, but to two concrete commitments with names of their own: Naturaleza Viva and Comunidad con Futuro.

We start with 1%. It's a starting point, not a ceiling. And every year we will publish the results here and on our social channels: how much was contributed, what it was used for, and what happened.

Commitment one

Naturaleza Viva.

We want to help regenerate forest and recover habitats for sloths and other species that are part of Costa Rica's natural wealth.

We will support restoration work with native trees, the recovery of connections between forest areas, and the care of places where wildlife finds food and shelter.

We will work with specialised partners so that every intervention answers the needs of the ecosystem and is followed over time. Our purpose is to help the forest grow, recover and go on being a home for life.

Commitment two

Comunidad con Futuro.

We want more people to find opportunities to grow where they live.

We will support training and development for people from nearby communities, with particular attention to young people and women who need an opportunity to get ahead: learning a trade, building their skills, finding decent work or strengthening a small business.

This commitment will also guide how we operate: creating local jobs, buying from producers in the area, and building partnerships so that the benefits of tourism reach more homes.

The two commitments meet.

We will seek to make forest recovery also generate training and work opportunities for the community, through activities such as native plant nurseries, maintenance of restored areas and environmental education.

In this way, caring for nature can become an opportunity to build a better future.

Native plant nurseries

Growing the tree that will be planted is also a trade to be learned and a job that stays in the area.

Caring for what's restored

Planting is the easy part. What really regenerates a forest is sustained care over time.

Environmental education

So the next generation here knows the ecosystem next door, and sees a future in it.

How we lead

Leadership that listens, serves and takes responsibility.

We believe in leadership that takes responsibility for the impact of its own growth. Leadership able to turn business vision into wellbeing for people and care for nature.

This is our vision of regenerative tourism: contributing to the recovery of the environment and the development of the community through everyday decisions and sustained action. Not through isolated gestures.

We will define concrete projects, work with partners and publish every year the resources contributed, the progress and the results, here and on our social channels. We want our commitment to be visible in forests that recover and in people who find new opportunities — and we want anyone to be able to check it.

Frequently asked questions.

What social and environmental commitments does Humaya have?
Two, with names of their own. Naturaleza Viva: regenerating forest and recovering habitats for sloths and other species. Comunidad con Futuro: training and opportunities for people from nearby communities, with particular attention to young people and women. We start by dedicating 1% of the hotel's revenue to both.
Are they already under way?
The hotel opens in December 2026, so the projects and partners are still being defined: when we have them, we will name them here. What is already decided is the commitment —1% of revenue— and how we will account for it: publishing the results every year on this site and on our social channels.
What is regenerative tourism?
Going beyond doing no harm. For us it means contributing to the recovery of the environment and the development of the community through everyday decisions and sustained action: local employment, buying from producers in the area, and 1% of revenue dedicated to the forest and to training people.
How does the guest take part?
Without having to do anything extra. Every stay will be part of that purpose: while you reconnect with what makes you human, your visit helps care for life and open possibilities for others.

Every stay will be part of that purpose.
