HUMAYA Experience

Feel what makes you human.

HUMAYA is born of a conviction: when we connect with what makes us human, we also awaken our capacity to care, to love and to contribute.

We create experiences that invite you to live.

We create experiences that invite us to move, to rest, to enjoy nature and to share with the people we love. Through our Momentos HUMAYA, we look after the small details and inspire new ways of enjoying life that can stay with our guests long after their stay.

And our purpose doesn't end with the guest: it also embraces the place that receives us and the people who call it home. See our two commitments.

This is not a schedule.

Almost every retreat hands you a timetable: yoga at six, breakfast at eight, the talk at ten. People leave their holiday exhausted. Humaya doesn't work that way.

HUMAYA Experience is the order behind everything here — not a list of things you have to do. Nobody will knock on your door. It has two layers: five pillars that hold the place up, and the Momentos HUMAYA, the small details scattered in between.

The structure

Five ways back to yourself.

  1. Move

    Trails, mountain and bike. The body first, always. The gym, the cold plunge and infrared light therapy live here: not as luxury, but as the tool that keeps a hard day from charging you the next one.

  2. Nourish

    Honest cooking, thermal water and deep rest. What comes out of the garden reaches the plate by the shortest route possible — and the cooking classes, if you want to learn to make it at home.

  3. Connect

    With people, with the forest, with yourself. Ten villas, not a hundred. Here we know your name, and that isn't a service detail: it's part of how the place is designed.

  4. Create

    Space, silence and fibre to really work and create. Because resting isn't always doing nothing: sometimes it's finally being able to think.

  5. Believe

    A place to ask the big questions again. It's the one nobody puts in a brochure, and the reason the hotel's slogan is Become More Human.

Momentos HUMAYA

The details you fall for.

If the five pillars are the structure, the Momentos are what happens inside it. Small details and experiences, scattered through the stay, that inspire new ways of caring for yourself and enjoying life. No timetable. No sign-up sheet. They just happen.

And the measure of whether they worked is simple: how many you take home and turn into a habit.

Moment

The first time in the cold

Nobody takes their first cold plunge alone. Someone stays, tells you how to breathe, and then goes quiet. Plenty of people leave doing it every week at home.

Moment

Before breakfast

Heading out for a walk without arranging anything with anyone, because the trail starts at the door. Half an hour. A different person comes back.

Moment

Cut it yourself

Going to the garden, cutting what you're about to eat, and cooking it. The distance from soil to plate, measured in steps.

Moment

Cold, heat, and again

The contrast between cold water and the Arenal's thermal water. You don't need to know anything: the body works it out.

Moment

Being remembered

Someone knowing you don't drink coffee. There being something to eat at eleven at night. Never having to ask twice.

Moment

The silence of ten villas

Ten, not a hundred. Silence isn't an absence of noise: it's the room where you can finally think.

World-class service means something concrete.

It means never asking twice. That if you arrive at eleven at night there's something to eat. That someone remembers you don't drink coffee. That if it's your first cold plunge, they don't leave you alone.

Ten villas make that possible. A hundred don't. The size of the hotel isn't a limitation we're papering over: it's the decision that makes everything else possible.

Frequently asked questions.

What exactly is HUMAYA Experience?
It's the framework we use to organise the stay. It has two layers: the five pillars —Move, Nourish, Connect, Create and Believe— and the Momentos HUMAYA, the small details scattered in between. It isn't a programme with timetables.
What are the Momentos HUMAYA?
Small details and experiences during the stay that inspire new ways of caring for yourself and enjoying life. They aren't scheduled activities: they're things that happen, and that many people end up taking home as a habit.
Is there a compulsory programme?
No. There are no compulsory timetables and no activities you have to be on time for. If all you want is to sleep for three days, that works too.
Does it work if I travel alone? And with family?
Both. The five pillars don't change; what changes is how much of each you want. Tell us how you're coming and we'll arrange it before you arrive.
When does Humaya Wellness Hotel open?
In December 2026, in La Fortuna de San Carlos, Costa Rica. The first reservations can already be made online.
